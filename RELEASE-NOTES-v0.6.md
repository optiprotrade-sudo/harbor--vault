# Harbor Vault v0.6 — APK-first dashboard update

## What's changed
- Replaces the large "100% Your Keys" dashboard banner with a compact total wallet balance card.
- Shows a **USD estimate**, with a clear **partial** label whenever even one listed balance or quote is unavailable.
- USD quotes come from CoinGecko's public `/simple/price` API, with NO seed phrases or wallet addresses in the price request.
- BTC, ETH, ERC-20 USDT, and LTC balance lookups operate on the FIRST receiving address only. LTC uses BlockCypher's Litecoin address endpoint and may be unsupported for some SegWit addresses; in that case the value is marked unavailable.
- Checks balance and prices when the app opens, whenever it resumes, every five minutes while foregrounded, and on manual refresh.
- Makes asset rows compact; tapping a row expands its receiving address and Copy/Receive actions.
- Adds a balance visibility button, last-checked time, and better error/refresh messages.
- Moves the self-custody explanation to the Security tab.
- Includes unit tests covering USD quotes, missing balances, partial totals, and LTC responses.

## Important safety and privacy limitations
- Receive-only, unaudited source prototype. NOT ready to receive real money or imported funded wallets.
- Total is an **estimate for successfully checked assets at address index 0**. It does NOT scan your wallet's other addresses or multiple accounts. Do not rely on the number as total holdings.
- Automatic blockchain balance checks transmit **public addresses** to third-party providers (mempool.space, publicnode.com, BlockCypher); these third parties may log IP and address associations. CoinGecko receives a price request, not your receiving address. Recovery words are not sent.
- CoinGecko's public API may return HTTP 429 / fail. If prices or balances are unavailable, Harbor Vault displays an unavailable or partial estimate instead of fabricating a number.
- USDT means Ethereum ERC-20 ONLY. Litecoin address lookup depends on BlockCypher compatibility with `ltc1` receiving addresses.
- A GitHub Actions build may use an ephemeral signing key; Android may reject an update over an older APK. DO NOT uninstall the previous version until its recovery words are securely backed up offline. Uninstalling may delete the locally encrypted wallet.
- No PIN/biometric lock, no send/sign, no multi-address discovery, no independent security audit.

## For the next APK build
- Use the patch archive for your existing repository (replace the files at the EXACT relative paths).
- Commit to `main` and run GitHub Actions > Build Harbor Vault Android APK > Run workflow.
- The artifact is generated by GitHub. The ZIP itself is Flutter source, not an installable APK.
